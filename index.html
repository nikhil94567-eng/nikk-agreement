FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to some common questions about our Rent Agreement services.

What is a Rent Agreement?
A Rent Agreement records the terms and conditions agreed between the landlord and tenant for renting a property.
How does the Rent Agreement process work?
You share the required details, the agreement draft is prepared, the details are reviewed and the applicable biometric and registration process is completed.
What details are generally required?
Generally, landlord, tenant and property-related details are required. Additional documents may be required depending on the applicable process.
Is doorstep biometric service available?
Doorstep biometric service may be available depending on location and applicable requirements. Please contact us to check availability.
How much does a Rent Agreement cost?
Government fees, registration charges and service charges may vary. Final applicable charges are communicated before the process is started.
How can I contact Nikk Rent Agreement?
You can call us directly or send us a WhatsApp message for assistance and to start your Rent Agreement process.
