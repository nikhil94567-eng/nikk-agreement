FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common Rent Agreement questions.

What is a Rent Agreement?

A Rent Agreement records the terms and conditions agreed between the landlord and tenant for renting a property.

How does the Rent Agreement process work?

You share the required details, the agreement draft is prepared, you review the details, biometric verification is completed where required, and the registration process is completed.

How long does the process take?

The time required can vary depending on document verification, biometric availability and registration requirements.

Is doorstep biometric service available?

Doorstep biometric service is available where applicable. Please call us to confirm availability for your location.
